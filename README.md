# Card Price
**Pokemon card scanner and price tracker. Scan binder pages and get all  cards identified with current market prices. Or search by name to look up any card's pricing and individual sales per condition.**

*STATUS: Post-LLM*

## Demos

### Scan a binder page

<p align="center">
  <img src="data/demo/scan_demo.gif" width="400" alt="Binder page upload to identified cards in seconds"/>
</p>

Upload a photo of a binder page. 
When the scan finishes, all nine cards come back labeled with current market prices for each condition. 
Tapping a card opens its detail page with sales history.

### Search any card

<p align="center">
  <img src="data/demo/search_demo.gif" width="320" alt="Search by name → autocomplete → result list → detail page"/>
</p>

Type a name and get autocomplete across every printing in the catalog. 
Tap a result for the full detail page: image, MP/LP/NM/HP/DMG prices, recent sales table, and a TCGplayer link.
